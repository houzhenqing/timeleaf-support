# Timeleaf · 那年此刻

Photo memories, collages, video stories and Live Photos for iPhone and iPad.

Find photos from the same day in past years, or choose your own photos and clips. Arrange 2–9 photos into a collage, add your words, or make a video story. Save your edits and return later.

## Getting started

1. Allow access to the photos you want to use to discover memories automatically, or choose individual items with the system photo picker.
2. Select a date to browse matching moments by year. Expand the date range if that day has no results.
3. Select at least two photos for a collage. In the editor, adjust the layout, crop, colors and text.
4. Choose the story output to play photos and clips in sequence, then export a video or a Live Photo.

## Common questions

**Why is a photo missing?** Check the selected date, date range and Photos permission in Settings. Limited access shows only allowed items. Items without a capture date cannot be matched to a historical day. iCloud-only items may need an internet connection to download through Apple.

**How do I play a Live Photo?** Save the newly created Live Photo to Photos, then touch and hold it there. Exports are 3 seconds and silent. Original Live Photos use their still frame when editing; this is not an original Live Photo converter.

**Can I return to an edit?** Saved stories stay on this device. Removing the app removes local stories and imported copies. Keep exported works and important originals separately.

**Does Timeleaf change my originals?** No. Editing creates new works. Timeleaf does not delete or modify original Photos items and does not upload your photos to a developer-operated service.

**Does an export include my location?** Exports omit source GPS metadata and do not add location labels or maps. Signs, captions or other location information already visible in the chosen photos remain visible.

## 中文支持

那年此刻帮你回看往年同日的照片与视频，也支持从相册自由选材，制作拼图、视频故事和实况照片。

遇到问题时，请[提交支持反馈](https://github.com/houzhenqing/timeleaf-support/issues/new)，说明系统版本、App 版本、操作步骤和遇到的问题。反馈页面公开可见，请勿上传私人照片、地址、账号信息等敏感内容。

- 没找到照片：检查日期、日期范围和照片访问权限；有限授权只显示允许访问的照片，缺少拍摄日期的素材无法按往年同日匹配。
- 云端素材：iCloud 照片可能需要由 Apple 联网下载。
- 实况播放：将新制作的实况照片保存到系统「照片」后，长按播放；导出的实况为 3 秒无声，编辑原有实况时使用静态帧。
- 继续编辑：作品保存在本机。卸载 App 会移除本地作品和导入副本，请另外保存重要原片和成品。
- 原片安全：不修改、不删除原照片，也不上传至开发者运营的服务。导出不带原始 GPS 元数据，不添加地点标签或地图；原照片画面中已有的地点信息仍会保留。
