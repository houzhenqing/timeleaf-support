# Timeleaf Privacy Policy / 那年今日隐私政策

Effective date: November 6, 2026. Publisher: Zhenqing Hou.

## Data collection

Timeleaf has no account system, advertising, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated photo upload service. The developer does not collect your photos, videos, photo coordinates, contacts, identifiers or usage events through the app. Editing and memory discovery take place on your device.

## Photos and permissions

You choose whether to allow photo-library access. Limited access uses only the items you authorize. Manual creation is also available through the system photo picker without full library access. The app uses selected images, clips, capture dates and, when present, photo coordinates to discover and edit moments. Photo coordinates may help group nearby moments locally; the app does not request or use your current device location.

Original Photos items are not modified or deleted. Saving a newly created Live Photo adds a new item only after you choose Save and permit adding photos. Exports omit source GPS metadata and do not add city names, location labels or maps. Information already visible in the original image or video remains visible.

## Local storage and retention

Saved stories contain edit instructions and references to selected media. Items imported with the system picker are copied into app storage. Temporary exports and local thumbnails may be cached to support editing and sharing. Deleting a story removes unused imported copies after a successful save, except when a recovery archive is retained to protect recovery. Recovery archives remain on the device until the app is removed. Deleting the app removes its local stories and imported copies, without deleting original Photos items. Device backups are governed by your Apple and device settings.

## Apple services and sharing

Apple may download authorized iCloud media through system Photos services. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy. When you choose to export or share, the destination you select receives the resulting file under that destination's policies. Opening a sharing sheet does not automatically post a work. Timeleaf does not upload your photo library to the developer.

## Your choices

Change or revoke Photos permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Delete local stories in the app, or remove the app to erase its local storage. No developer account needs to be deleted because the app has no account system.

## Support and this website

For privacy questions, use [the support page](https://github.com/houzhenqing/timeleaf-support). This site is hosted by GitHub, which processes website traffic under its own privacy policy. Support issues are public. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Information you voluntarily submit through support is used to respond to that request.

## Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above.

## 中文隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 6 日。开发者：Zhenqing Hou。

**数据收集**：那年今日无需账号，不含广告、追踪 SDK、统计 SDK 或开发者运营的照片上传服务。开发者不会通过 App 收集你的照片、视频、照片坐标、通讯录、标识符或使用事件。回忆发现和编辑在设备本机完成。

**照片与权限**：由你决定是否授权读取图库。有限授权仅访问你允许的素材；也可通过系统选择器手动选材，无需读取整个图库。App 使用选中素材的画面、拍摄日期和已有照片坐标进行本地回忆整理与编辑，不申请、不使用设备当前位置。

**原片与导出**：不修改或删除原始照片。只有你主动保存并允许添加照片后，App 才会新增制作好的实况照片。导出文件不带原始 GPS 元数据，不添加城市名、地点标签或地图。原画面中已有的文字或地点信息仍会保留。

**本机存储**：作品保存编辑指令和素材引用；经系统选择器导入的文件会复制到 App 存储中。临时导出和缩略图可能用于编辑、分享和加载。删除作品并成功保存后，会清理不再使用的导入副本；存在恢复备份时，为保护恢复能力会保留副本。恢复备份保留在设备上，卸载 App 后移除。卸载会移除本地作品和导入副本，不会删除系统「照片」中的原片。设备备份由你的 Apple 与设备设置决定。

**系统服务与分享**：Apple 可能通过系统照片服务下载已授权的 iCloud 素材，适用 Apple 隐私政策。主动选择分享目标后，目标服务会按其政策接收成品；打开分享面板不会自动发布。App 不会将图库上传给开发者。

**你的选择**：可随时在系统设置中调整或撤回照片权限，在 App 内删除作品，或卸载 App 以移除本地存储。App 没有账号，因此没有需要注销的开发者服务账号。

**反馈与本页面**：隐私问题可通过[支持页](https://github.com/houzhenqing/timeleaf-support)反馈。本页面由 GitHub 托管，网站访问适用 GitHub 隐私政策。支持反馈公开可见，请勿提交敏感个人信息；你主动提交的反馈仅用于处理该请求。

如数据处理方式发生变化，本政策会同步更新，并注明生效日期。
